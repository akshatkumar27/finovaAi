FinovaAI — v3 preview · design language + hero screens

App Read

Personal-finance goal tracker for young Indian professionals. Calm, competent, personal — Monarch × Wise × Copilot Money. One accent, muted semantics, editorial numeric hierarchy.

Anti-defaults ruled out

× indigo/violet gradient hero  ·  × generic teal money color
× emoji as icons in list rows  ·  × "Welcome back, [name] 👋"
× 5-tab bar with center FAB  ·  × stack of pure-white rounded cards on every screen

Palette

Warm cream canvas (not sterile #FFF). Deep muted teal-forest accent (wealth without cliche). Desaturated semantics so warnings and losses don't scream.
